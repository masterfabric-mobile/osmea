How to test the app (detailed steps)

1) Launch and first run
   • Open the app. On first launch you may see onboarding screens (swipe through or tap Skip/Get Started).
   • If you see a login screen, go to step 2. If you land on the home screen, you can browse as guest or tap Account/Profile to sign in (step 2).

2) Sign in with demo account
   • Tap the account/profile icon (e.g. person icon in tab bar or app bar).
   • Tap “Sign in” or “Log in”.
   • Enter the Username and Password from “Sign-in credentials” above.
   • Tap Sign in. You should land on Home or Account.

3) Browse and search
   • Home: Scroll the home screen (banners, deals of the day, categories, recommended products).
   • Categories: Tap a category on home or open the categories/catalog section. Tap a category to open the product list.
   • Search: Tap the search bar, type a product name or keyword (e.g. “shirt”, “bag”), tap Search. Confirm results load.
   • Product list: Tap a product to open its detail page.

4) Product detail and cart
   • On a product detail screen: Check image, title, price, description. If the product has options (size, color), select them.
   • Tap “Add to cart” (optionally change quantity). Confirm the cart badge or count updates.
   • Open Cart (cart icon). Check that the item appears with correct price and quantity. Optionally add another product from home or search and confirm both appear in the cart.
   • Coupon: In the cart screen, find the coupon/promo field, enter a valid test coupon if you have one, apply it. Confirm the total or discount updates.

5) Checkout (no real charge)
   • In Cart, tap “Checkout” or “Proceed to checkout”.
   • Shipping/Address: Select or add a shipping address. If the demo account has saved addresses, select one; otherwise add a test address and continue.
   • Shipping method: Select a shipping option and continue.
   • Payment: If the app uses a test/sandbox payment method, select it to complete the order. Otherwise, you can verify the flow up to the payment screen and stop there (no need to enter real card details).
   • Summary: Confirm order summary (items, totals, address) is correct before the final “Place order” step.

6) Orders and account
   • Orders: From the account/profile area, open “Order history” or “My orders”. Confirm the list loads (and the new order appears if you completed checkout).
   • Order detail: Tap an order to see status, items, and totals.
   • Profile: In account, open “My profile” or “Edit profile”. Confirm you can view (and optionally edit) name, email, or other profile fields.
   • Addresses: Open “Addresses” or “Saved addresses”. Confirm saved addresses list; you can add or edit an address to verify the form.

7) Wishlist
   • From home or product detail, tap the heart/wishlist icon on a product to add it to the wishlist.
   • Open the Wishlist (e.g. from tab bar or account). Confirm the product appears. Remove one item to verify the list updates.

8) Optional checks
   • Campaigns: If home or a menu shows a campaign/banner, tap it and confirm the campaign or linked product opens.
   • Language: If the app supports multiple languages, change language in Settings and confirm the main screens update.
   • Logout: From account/profile, tap “Sign out” or “Log out”. Confirm you are returned to login or guest home. Sign in again with the demo credentials to confirm login works.

If anything fails (e.g. login error, blank product list, crash on checkout), please note the step and device/iOS version in the review notes so we can address it.
